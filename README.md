### 📊 Turning Hotel Reviews into Actionable Business Insights  
This project uses **AI, NLP, and Business Intelligence (Power BI)** to analyze hotel reviews and understand how guest sentiment impacts hotel performance and revenue.

---

## 🚀 Project Highlights  
✅ Sentiment Analysis of Hotel Reviews  
✅ Topic Mining (Guest complaints, praise categories)  
✅ Hotel Performance Metrics & Customer Feedback Correlation  
✅ Interactive Power BI Dashboard  
✅ Insights & Recommendations for Hotel Strategy

## 🎯 Objective  
To help hotels **improve guest satisfaction & revenue** by analyzing patterns in customer feedback and business KPIs using AI.

---

## 📂 Dataset Information  
- Customer reviews  
- Ratings & sentiment indicators  
- Hotel performance metrics  
- Operational attributes (service, cleanliness, pricing, etc.)

> *Dataset was used only for educational & research purpose under Infosys Springboard Program.*

## 🧠 AI & Analytics Workflow  

1️⃣ Data Cleaning  
2️⃣ Text Pre-processing  
3️⃣ Sentiment Analysis (NLP)  
4️⃣ Topic Classification (Key complaint reasons)  
5️⃣ KPI Analysis (Occupancy, Rating, Pricing, etc.)  
6️⃣ Dashboard Report in Power BI
